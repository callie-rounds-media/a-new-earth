What we already know

The verified part, so neither of us has to go looking. Everything below has a source you can click.

the operation

Rhinos are darted from a helicopter, the ground team covers the eyes and ears, a chainsaw or hand saw takes off roughly 90 to 93 percent of the horn, and the stump is smoothed and sealed with tar before the reversal drug. Save the Rhino

the pace

Experienced teams have dehorned up to 22 rhinos in a single day, and one reserve did 42 over three days. A whole population can be done in one filmable window. Africa Geographic · Aspinall Foundation

the cycle

Horn grows back, so reserves repeat the procedure every 18 to 24 months. That makes this a repeatable project, not a one-off. Save the Rhino

the science

A June 2025 study in Science across eight Greater Kruger reserves found dehorning cut poaching by 78 percent while using about 1.2 percent of the anti-poaching budget. That is the line a funder can put in a press release. University of Oxford

the door

Blake's October 2026 South Africa trip already lists rhino horn trimming at Manyoni Private Game Reserve with Zululand Conservation Trust and Wild Tomorrow. The relationship exists, we would be adding a helicopter, a crew, and a funder. The SOSA
